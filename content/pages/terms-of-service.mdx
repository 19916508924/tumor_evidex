## Evidence support, not medical advice

Evidex organizes reviewed sources into traceable evidence outputs for professional evaluation. It does not diagnose disease, prescribe treatment, determine eligibility, or replace clinical judgment. Users must independently review cited sources, current labels, trial records, patient context, and applicable rules before making a clinical or operational decision.

The public demo has a deliberately limited set of supported diseases, biomarkers, and variants. A result, missing result, rank, or summary must not be interpreted as a complete review of all available evidence.

## Authorized use
